About us

A cafe for coffee people and culture people.

We are a cafe and shop located in the vibrant city of Manila, dedicated to exploring the connection between coffee and culture.

You’ll find us on Escolta, pouring a Philippine blend among the makers, artists and small shops of HUB Make Lab. Come for the espresso. Stay for a plate of fresh pasta and whatever is happening on the floor that day.

About the space

  • Philippine blend

    Every espresso is a double shot of our Philippine blend, roasted by Escolta Coffee Company.

  • Made from scratch

    Pesto, mushroom gravy and chai are made by hand, not poured from a packet.

  • Here or to go

    Dine in at the hub, take it out onto Escolta, or have it delivered.

    The space

    Inside a 1928 Art Deco landmark.

    Find us inside the HUB Make Lab, a culture and creative hub, situated within the historic First United Building on Escolta Street in Manila.

    1. 1928

      The building opens, designed by Andrés Luna de San Pedro. On completion it is the tallest in Manila.

    2. 1936

      Berg’s Department Store sets up shop on the ground floor and becomes an Escolta landmark.

    3. 2016

      HUB Make Lab opens as a home for creative startups, and The Den starts pouring.
